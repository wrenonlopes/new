# BIOD comedy ad briefs — v1, 2026-09-13

For BIOD's Claude ads agents. Companion to `uae-competitive-state.json`.

**The premise, in one line:** BIOD's homepage is already funny and already claims-cleared.
Its seven live ads are not. Every hook below is lifted from copy that is *already on the site*,
so nothing here needs new fact-checking — only new edits.

Market context that makes this work: Bambuyu has run **4 ads all-time**, all sincere
("A Fresh Towel Every Time ✨"). BIOD has run **7**, the boldest of which — *"Why use that dirty
towel on your pretty face?"* — is a scold, not a joke. Nobody on this shelf is running real
creative volume. Ship 15–20 variants and BIOD out-iterates the entire UAE category in a month.

---

## Rules before you write a single frame

**Claims.** Only three claims are cleared (root `README.md`): the 89% coliform / 1-in-4 E. coli
towel study (Food Protection Trends 34(5), 2014), AAD towel-hygiene guidance, and bamboo
growth/biodegradability. **Never** "antibacterial fabric", never invented trial percentages.
A joke is not a loophole — if the punchline asserts a fact, that fact must be on the cleared list.

**Never name Bambuyu or any competitor.** Punch at the *cotton towel* and the *wet wipe*. The
enemy is an object, not a company. Naming a rival invites a legal reply and makes BIOD look
small; mocking a damp towel makes BIOD look confident.

**UAE comedy guardrails.** This is the part most agencies get wrong. Keep humour on the towel,
the bathroom, and the self-deprecating narrator. Stay entirely away from religion, politics,
national identity, modesty/undress, alcohol, and anything that could read as mocking a person
rather than an object. Self-deprecation and absurdist object-comedy travel safely in the GCC;
edgy body or relationship humour does not. If a line would make a family group chat wince,
cut it. Also: plan a tonal step-down for Ramadan — loud mockery reads badly in that window.

**Register.** BIOD is the funny friend who is right, not the brand shouting. Dry beats loud.

## Brief 01 — "Damp Since Tuesday" *(lead concept)*

- **Cleared line:** `damp since Tuesday` — already live in the Versus section.
- **Hook (0–2s):** hard cut to a limp bathroom towel. Lower-third title card, deadpan:
  **"Damp since Tuesday."** No voiceover yet. The silence does the work.
- **Beats:** (2–5s) slow push-in on the towel like a true-crime doc subject. (5–8s) narrator,
  flat: "This is where you put your clean face. Every morning." (8–12s) hand pulls a fresh BIOD
  sheet — the only bright, dry, crisp thing in the frame. (12–15s) title: *fresh sheet, every wash.*
- **CTA:** "Dhs 19. One week." → PDP
- **Testing:** deadpan documentary vs. the current scold. This is the direct A/B against
  *"Why use that dirty towel on your pretty face?"*
- **Why it should win:** it mocks the towel, not the viewer. The current ad blames the customer
  for their breakouts; this one gives them a villain to blame instead. *(Inference — test it.)*

## Brief 02 — "Bacteria's Airbnb"

- **Cleared line:** `bacteria's airbnb` — live in the Versus stat block.
- **Hook (0–2s):** a cheerful property-listing card over a photo of a crumpled towel:
  **"Bacteria's Airbnb ★★★★★ — Superhost."**
- **Beats:** listing-style bullets appear — "Warm. Damp. Never cleaned. Sleeps millions."
  Then the record-scratch: the 89% statistic on screen **with the citation visible**
  (Food Protection Trends, 2014). Cut to BIOD: "Checkout is permanent."
- **CTA:** "See the receipts" → `/#receipts`
- **Testing:** whether a joke can carry a citation. This is BIOD's structural moat — no
  competitor can put a peer-reviewed source on screen without inventing one.
- **Note:** the parody must read as obvious parody. No real brand marks, no logos.

## Brief 03 — "Worst Case, You're Out a Shawarma" *(the local moat)*

- **Cleared line:** `Dhs 19. One week. Clearer mornings. Worst case, you're out a shawarma.`
  — already live in the final CTA.
- **Hook (0–2s):** split screen. Left: a shawarma. Right: the Dhs 19 BIOD trial. Title:
  **"Same price. One of them fixes your face."**
- **Beats:** narrator runs the maths with total sincerity, as if presenting a serious investment
  case. Land on the existing line verbatim. Small beat of silence. Then: "Worst case, you're out
  a shawarma."
- **CTA:** "Dhs 19 · one week" → trial SKU
- **Why this is the most defensible ad on the list:** it is UAE-specific humour. Clean Skin Club
  and TSC structurally cannot write this line. Bambuyu's sincere eco-register cannot either. Run
  this one in Arabic as well.

## Brief 04 — "Immortal (Derogatory)"

- **Cleared line:** `immortal (derogatory)` + `Your plastic wipes will outlive your grandkids. Sit with that.`
- **Hook (0–2s):** a wet wipe on a plinth, lit like a museum artifact. Card: **"Immortal."**
  Beat. Second card, smaller: **"(derogatory)"**
- **Beats:** mock-museum placard energy — "Est. 2026. Expected retirement: 2126." Then the
  grandkids line, delivered flat. Cut to bamboo: "Ours is gone in months."
- **CTA:** "Shop bamboo" → collection
- **Claims note:** bamboo biodegradability is cleared. Do not put a specific decomposition
  figure on screen unless it is the one already cited in the `planet` section.

## Brief 05 — "The Group Chat" *(uses an existing site device)*

- **Cleared device:** the homepage `chat` section — `skincare gc`, "maya is online",
  `Respectfully, your towel is disgusting.`
- **Hook (0–2s):** phone screen, typing indicator, then the message lands:
  **"respectfully your towel is disgusting"**
- **Beats:** the reply bubbles escalate — one friend defensive, one friend already converted,
  screenshot of the 89% stat pasted into the chat. Ends with the convert: "my shelf's new main
  character" (also existing copy).
- **CTA:** soft — "join the gc" → PDP
- **Testing:** native-feeling social format vs. produced ad. Cheapest of the five to shoot;
  strongest candidate for creator-made versions.

## Ship plan

| Wave | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Briefs 01 + 03, 3 variants each (hook swaps only) | Deadpan vs local-humour, head to head |
| 2 | Winner's hook × 5 new openings; add 02 | Scale the winning register, test the citation joke |
| 3 | Brief 05 in creator hands (2k–50k followers, UAE) | The documented category engine, in BIOD's voice |
| 4 | Arabic cuts of the wave-1 winner + 03 | Unclaimed surface, no competitor present |

**Volume target:** 15–20 live variants inside 30 days. The bar is embarrassingly low — the
entire UAE shelf has produced 11 ads between BIOD and Bambuyu combined, all-time.

**Kill criteria:** judge hooks on 3-second retention, not likes. A comedy ad that gets laughs
and no clicks is a failed ad — the joke has to be *about the product's reason to exist*, which
is why every brief above lands on the towel, never on a random gag.

## What to feed back into the next run

Per variant, log: hook text, register (deadpan / absurdist / self-deprecating / local),
3-second retention, CTR, CVR, and whether the punchline carried a cleared claim. That turns
`uae-competitive-state.json` from a snapshot into a trend, and tells us which comedic register
the UAE market actually rewards — which nobody currently knows, BIOD included.
